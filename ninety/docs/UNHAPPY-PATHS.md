# Unhappy paths, and what we say

Generated by `node scripts/generate-unhappy-paths.mjs` from the message catalogues the product actually ships (`apps/api/src/i18n/catalogues/*.json`). Do not edit by hand — edit the copy and regenerate, or the document and the product will disagree and the launch team will quote the document.

The rule behind all of it: the handling is not the feature, the message is. A buyer who receives "Error: 409" decides the product is broken and tells the other workshops.

## 1. Zero offers at the response deadline

**What happens.** The search widens to tier 2: the stock-profile weight is dropped, the radius is extended by half, and lower-scored yards are included. The buyer is told, with the new deadline as an absolute time.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/matching/hooks.ts — onResponseDeadline`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "zero offers at the response deadline"`

**`notify.buyer.search_widened`**

> Nobody has answered on {{reference}} yet, so we have widened the search to more yards and a larger area. We will tell you either way by {{deadline}}.

> لم يردّ أحد على {{reference}} حتى الآن، لذلك وسّعنا البحث ليشمل محلات أكثر ونطاقًا أوسع. سنخبرك بالنتيجة في الحالتين قبل {{deadline}}.

## 2. Zero offers after the widened search

**What happens.** The request closes honestly, a demand miss is logged with the part, the vehicle, the place and the time, and the buyer is told plainly that nobody had it. Never silence.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/matching/hooks.ts — onWideningDeadline`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "zero offers after widening"`

**`notify.buyer.no_offers`**

> We could not find {{part}} for your {{vehicle}} today. Nobody who stocks it had it on the shelf. We have logged it — if it comes in, we will tell you.

> لم نتمكن من إيجاد {{part}} لسيارتك {{vehicle}} اليوم. لم تتوفر لدى أي محل على الرف. سجّلنا الطلب — وإذا توفرت القطعة سنخبرك.

**`notify.buyer.no_supply`**

> No yard in your area breaks the {{vehicle}}, so we had nobody to ask for {{part}}. We have logged it.

> لا يوجد محل في منطقتك يفكّك سيارات {{vehicle}}، لذلك لم نجد من نسأله عن {{part}}. سجّلنا الطلب.

## 3. A yard withdraws an offer before the buyer chooses

**What happens.** The offer disappears from the buyer's list; the others are untouched. No score penalty — withdrawing an open quote is honest, and punishing it produces yards who quote on stock they do not have.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/routes/supplier.ts — POST /v1/supplier/offers/:id/withdraw`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "supplier withdraws after offering"`

**`notify.buyer.offer_withdrawn`**

> One of the offers on {{reference}} was withdrawn by the yard. Your remaining offers are still open.

> سحب أحد المحلات عرضه على {{reference}}. بقية العروض ما زالت متاحة.

**`unhappy.supplier_withdrew_before_accept`**

> A yard withdrew its offer before you chose. The rest are unaffected.

> سحب أحد المحلات عرضه قبل أن تختار. بقية العروض لم تتأثر.

## 4. A yard withdraws an offer the buyer had already accepted

**What happens.** The card authorisation is voided immediately, the request returns to selection with the other offers still open, and the yard takes a score penalty — the buyer was let down after committing.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/matching/hooks.ts — onOfferWithdrawn`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "supplier withdraws an ACCEPTED offer"`

**`notify.buyer.accepted_offer_withdrawn`**

> The yard withdrew the offer you accepted on {{reference}}. Nothing has been charged and the hold is released — your other offers are open again.

> سحب المحل العرض الذي قبلته على {{reference}}. لم يُخصم أي مبلغ وأُلغي الحجز — وعروضك الأخرى متاحة من جديد.

## 5. The buyer cancels

**What happens.** Defined in every state. Any held authorisation is voided BEFORE the state changes, so a failure to void leaves the request visibly open rather than closed and wrong. Which message is sent depends on whether a driver already has the part.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/requests/routes.ts — POST /v1/requests/:id/cancel`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "the buyer cancels"`

**`unhappy.buyer_cancelled_authorised`**

> You cancelled {{reference}}. The hold on your card has been released — nothing was taken.

> ألغيت {{reference}}. أُلغي الحجز على بطاقتك — ولم يُخصم أي مبلغ.

**`unhappy.buyer_cancelled_dispatched`**

> You cancelled {{reference}} after a driver had collected it. We have released the hold and our team will contact you about the return.

> ألغيت {{reference}} بعد أن استلم السائق القطعة. أُلغي الحجز وسيتواصل معك فريقنا بشأن الإرجاع.

## 6. The card is declined

**What happens.** The request stays in selection with every offer intact. Fifteen minutes of supplier effort is not thrown away because one card failed; the buyer picks another card or another offer.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/payments/service.ts — acceptOfferAndAuthorise`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "the card is declined"`

**`error.payment_failed`**

> Your card was not authorised. No money has been taken.

> لم تتم الموافقة على بطاقتك. لم يُخصم أي مبلغ.

**`notify.buyer.payment_failed`**

> Your card was not authorised for {{reference}}. No money has been taken and your offers are still there — try another card or another offer.

> لم تتم الموافقة على بطاقتك لـ {{reference}}. لم يُخصم أي مبلغ وعروضك ما زالت متاحة — جرّب بطاقة أخرى أو عرضًا آخر.

## 7. The courier fails after collection

**What happens.** The part physically exists and is in a van. An ops escalation is raised with the request attached, the buyer is told honestly and promised a call, and the yard takes NO score penalty — they did their job.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/logistics/service.ts — onCourierFailure`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "the courier fails after collection"`

**`notify.buyer.courier_failed_after_collection`**

> Your part for {{reference}} has been collected but the delivery has run into a problem. It is safe, our team is tracking it, and we will call you within the hour.

> تم استلام قطعتك الخاصة بـ {{reference}} لكن التوصيل واجه مشكلة. القطعة بأمان، وفريقنا يتابعها، وسنتصل بك خلال ساعة.

**`notify.buyer.courier_all_failed`**

> No driver has accepted {{reference}} yet, so a member of our team is arranging one directly. New expected time {{eta}}. Nothing has been charged.

> لم يقبل أي سائق طلب {{reference}} حتى الآن، لذلك يتولى أحد أفراد فريقنا ترتيب السائق مباشرة. الوقت المتوقع الجديد {{eta}}. لم يُخصم أي مبلغ.

## 8. The part arrives damaged or wrong

**What happens.** A dispute, raised by either side, with evidence from both. Only an admin resolves it. The outcome is expressed as what the buyer ends up paying; the money moves by refund, void or reduced capture depending on where it already is.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/disputes/service.ts — resolveDispute`

**Proved by.** `tests/disputes.test.ts`

**`notify.buyer.dispute_opened`**

> We have opened a case on {{reference}}. Someone will come back to you within one working day.

> فتحنا شكوى بخصوص {{reference}}. سيتواصل معك أحدهم خلال يوم عمل واحد.

**`notify.buyer.dispute_resolved`**

> Your case on {{reference}} has been resolved: {{resolution}}

> تم البتّ في شكواك بخصوص {{reference}}: {{resolution}}

**`notify.buyer.refund_issued`**

> {{amount}} has been refunded on {{reference}}. It normally shows on your statement within a few working days.

> تم استرجاع مبلغ {{amount}} على {{reference}}. يظهر عادةً في كشف حسابك خلال أيام عمل قليلة.

## 9. The buyer never confirms receipt

**What happens.** The order auto-confirms after the market's own window (24 hours in the UAE) and the card is captured. The buyer is told it happened and that they can still raise a problem.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/logistics/service.ts — autoConfirmDelivery`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "the buyer never confirms"`

**`notify.buyer.auto_confirmed`**

> {{reference}} was completed automatically after 24 hours. Payment of {{total}} has been taken. If something was wrong, you can still raise it with us.

> اكتمل {{reference}} تلقائيًا بعد أربع وعشرين ساعة. تم خصم مبلغ {{total}}. إن كان هناك خطأ، ما زال بإمكانك إبلاغنا.

## 10. The same request is posted twice

**What happens.** Detected and refused with a warning naming the existing request, not silently fanned out to the same yards a second time. The buyer can post it again deliberately once warned.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/requests/service.ts — duplicate detection`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "a duplicate is detected"`

**`error.duplicate_request`**

> You posted a very similar request a few minutes ago. Post it again anyway, or open the one you already have.

> لقد نشرت طلبًا مشابهًا جدًا قبل دقائق. يمكنك نشره مرة أخرى على أي حال، أو فتح الطلب الموجود لديك.

**`unhappy.duplicate_detected`**

> This looks like a request you already posted {{minutes}} minutes ago. Posting it again will send it to the same yards a second time.

> يبدو أن هذا طلب نشرته قبل {{minutes}} دقيقة. نشره مرة أخرى سيُرسله إلى المحلات نفسها مرة ثانية.

## 11. A terminal is offline when the job goes out

**What happens.** Recorded as "unreachable", never as "no response". The score delta for unreachable is exactly zero. Penalising a yard for a power cut is the fastest way to lose the yards that took three months to sign.

**Where.** `apps/api/src/matching/hooks.ts — markNonResponders`

**Proved by.** `tests/unhappy-paths.test.ts — "a terminal offline at fan-out"`

**`notify.supplier.offline_not_penalised`**

> Your terminal was offline when {{reference}} went out, so it has not counted against your response rate.

> كان جهازك غير متصل عند إرسال {{reference}}، لذلك لم يُحتسب ذلك ضد نسبة استجابتك.

**`unhappy.terminal_offline_at_fanout`**

> Your terminal was offline when this job went out, so you did not miss it and it has not affected your score.

> كان جهازك غير متصل عند إرسال هذا الطلب، لذلك لم تفوّته ولم يؤثر على تقييمك.

## 12. A yard tries to put contact details in a note

**What happens.** Rejected, never silently mangled, in Latin and Arabic-Indic numerals alike — and the yard is told WHY the rule exists, because a supplier who only sees a refusal concludes the product is broken.

**Where.** `packages/shared/src/scrub.ts, apps/terminal/src/components/QuoteScreen.tsx`

**Proved by.** `packages/shared — scrub tests; the RTL pass captures the screen`

**`scrub.rejected`**

> Your note could not be sent because it contains contact details.

> تعذّر إرسال ملاحظتك لأنها تحتوي على بيانات تواصل.

**`scrub.why`**

> NINETY keeps both sides anonymous. That is what protects your next job from being taken off the platform — and it is why we pay you automatically and handle the delivery. Please remove the contact details and send the note again.

> ناينتي تُبقي الطرفين مجهولَين. هذا ما يحمي طلبك القادم من أن يخرج خارج المنصة — وهو سبب دفعنا لك تلقائيًا وتولّينا التوصيل. يرجى حذف بيانات التواصل وإرسال الملاحظة مرة أخرى.
